About

One app, instead of five tools and a front desk.

LeadQ is one AI assistant for your whole business. It answers WhatsApp, SMS, web chat, email and your phone line, and it books the appointment while you work.

Why we built it

Every missed message is a job someone else books.

Local businesses lose real money in the gap between a customer reaching out and someone getting back to them. It happens at midnight, on a Sunday, and in the twenty minutes you were with another customer. Answering machines, missed calls and unread texts don't add up to a lost message. They add up to a lost booking.

What we believe

You shouldn't need five tools to answer a customer.

Most small teams stitch together a chat widget, a texting app, an inbox, a booking tool and a receptionist, and still drop messages. We think it should be one assistant that knows your business, speaks in your voice, and works every channel the same way. So that's what we built.

What LeadQ is

An app you run yourself.

Not an agency, not a done-for-you service. You set it up from your phone in minutes, it learns your services and your tone, and it starts answering. You stay in control, and you can jump into any conversation whenever you want.

Every channel

WhatsApp, SMS, web chat, email, and your phone line on Pro. One assistant across all of them.

Books the work

It checks real availability, books, reschedules, and sends the reminder so fewer people no-show.

Sounds like you

It learns your services, policies and voice, so replies read like your business, not a bot.
