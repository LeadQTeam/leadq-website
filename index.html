Questions, answered.

What's a credit?

Credits are how LeadQ measures usage. Everything the assistant does on every channel draws from one monthly pool, so there's no per-channel math to do. Run low and you top up in one tap. WhatsApp's own conversation fees are billed by Meta, on your WhatsApp Business account.

What happens if I run out of credits?

Your assistant stops replying until your pool resets or you top up. Topping up takes one tap in the app, and it picks straight back up.

Is there a contract?

No. Plans are month to month, and you can cancel whenever you want from inside the app. If you cancel, we release any phone numbers on the account.

Do I need to register for texting?

In the US, business texting needs A2P registration. We guide you through it inside the app. Canada doesn't need it, and the UAE runs on WhatsApp instead.

Can I keep my WhatsApp number?

Yes. Connect a new number or bring the one your customers already message.

Can I take over a conversation?

Any time. Pause the assistant for one contact or a whole channel, reply yourself, then hand it back.
