## What LeadQ actually is

One AI assistant that answers a small business's incoming messages, on every channel they use,
and books the appointment while they work. It is not a chatbot bolted to a website — it is the
same assistant across WhatsApp, SMS, web chat, email and (in North America) the phone line, with
one shared memory of the business and one inbox for the owner.

**The one-sentence version:** it answers every message in seconds, day or night, and books the
job — so the owner stops losing customers in the gap between "they messaged" and "someone
replied".

**Who it is for.** Small local businesses where the owner is the bottleneck: dental and aesthetic
clinics, salons and barbers, home services (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, cleaning), real estate.
The common thread is someone with their hands full who cannot answer the phone or a text.

**The problem it removes.** A customer messages at 9pm on a Sunday, or while the owner is with
another customer. Nobody replies for four hours. The customer books with whoever answered first.
That is the whole pitch, and it is worth leading with the moment, not the technology.

## What it does

**Answers, on every channel at once.** WhatsApp, SMS, web chat, email, and the phone line on Pro
in North America. Same assistant, same knowledge, one inbox.

**Books into the real calendar.** It checks genuine availability, respects the business's booking
rules (notice, buffers, how far ahead), books, and sends confirmations and reminders. Reminders
are where no-shows disappear. Google and Outlook calendar sync.

**Knows the business.** Hours, services, prices, insurance, parking, policies — whatever the
owner teaches it. It can read their website and suggest answers, and nothing goes live until the
owner approves it.

**Follows up.** A lead goes quiet, it nudges them on a schedule, and never during quiet hours.

**Hands over when it should.** The owner writes the rules — upset customer, asks for a refund,
wants a person, anything it is unsure about. The conversation then waits for a human, and the
assistant stops. On the website widget specifically, a business can switch this off, because
anyone can walk up to a web chat and ask for a person.

**Stays under control.** Pause it on one channel or all of them, for an hour or until they say
so. Take over any conversation by hand and hand it back.

**The AI Receptionist (Pro, North America only).** It answers the phone in the assistant's own
voice, books live against the calendar, and warm-transfers when it matters. Nearly 40 voices.

**Insights (Pro).** What moved, which channel books, when people write, and who needed a human.

### Credits, in plain words

Credits are one pool covering everything the assistant does, so there is no per-channel maths.
Roughly: an AI conversation is 80 credits, a text message about 15, an email 1, a minute of voice
220. **WhatsApp messages cost no LeadQ credits** — Meta bills WhatsApp conversation fees directly
to the client's own WhatsApp Business account, which is worth saying because for inbound leads
those fees are usually near zero.

Credits reset each billing cycle. Extra credits can be bought as packs, they never expire, and a
business can set automatic top-ups with a floor and a monthly spending cap so it cannot run away.

**In posts, talk in conversations, not credits.** "About 550 conversations a month" means
something; "45,000 credits" does not.

## Do NOT say these things

Every one of these is false, and each has a reason.

- ❌ **"Download the LeadQ app."** There is no mobile app. It runs in a browser on any device.
  The mobile app is **coming soon** — you may say that, but never imply it exists, and never use
  App Store or Google Play badges.
- ❌ **Anything about voice, calls, phone numbers or SMS to a UAE audience.** Not "coming soon",
  not "not yet" — not at all. AI voice is not permitted there.
- ❌ **"Free forever" or "free plan".** There is a free trial and 10 free replies, not a free tier.
- ❌ **A price without its market**, or a converted price. Each market has its own list.
- ❌ **"Unlimited"** anything. Every plan has a credit pool.
- ❌ **"Replaces your receptionist" / "fires your staff."** It answers what nobody got to, and
  hands over when a person is needed. Sell the gap it covers, not the job it takes.
- ❌ **Medical, legal or financial claims** on behalf of a clinic client.
- ❌ **Named clients or real customer data.** No screenshots of real conversations.
- ❌ **Specific outcome numbers** — "cuts no-shows by 40%", "3x your bookings". We have not
  measured those. Describe the mechanism instead: it confirms and reminds, which is where
  no-shows disappear.
- ❌ **"Instant" or "24/7 human support."** Priority support is a Pro feature and it is not a
  live human at 3am.

## Tone

The product's own voice: plain, warm, specific, never hyped. It talks about the moment a customer
gets missed, not about "AI-powered omnichannel engagement". Short sentences. Concrete scenes — a
patient messaging at 11pm, a barber with their hands full, a plumber on a roof.

Good: *"Your customer messaged at 9pm. Someone answered."*
Bad: *"Leverage AI to maximise conversion."*

Avoid: revolutionary, game-changer, unlock, supercharge, seamless, cutting-edge, 10x.
